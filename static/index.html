Deferred Salary Calculator

Employees are paid less than their fictional salary; the difference is deferred. Set the startup's success probability for each year and add your employees – the totals update as you type.

1 Years & success probability

Chance the startup succeeds in each year (must be above 0%). The deferred salary of that year is divided by it to compensate for the risk, e.g. 20% success means 5× the deferred salary.

2 Employees

Start year 1 means from the very beginning. The fictional and actual salaries are annual gross and apply in every year from the start year on.
